Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the calorie detection? +
Our AI model provides estimates based on visual recognition with 70-85% accuracy for common foods. For best results, use clear, well-lit photos of single food items.
How do I calculate my BMI? +
Navigate to the BMI Calculator tab, enter your height in centimeters and weight in kilograms, then click "Calculate BMI". Your result will be saved to your profile automatically.
What analytics are available? +
You can view calorie trends across daily, weekly, monthly, and annual periods. Analytics include total calories, average per entry, food frequency breakdown, and recent entries.
Is my data secure? +
Yes. Passwords are encrypted using secure hashing algorithms. Your personal data is stored locally and never shared with third parties.
What foods can the AI recognize? +
The AI recognizes common foods including fruits, vegetables, prepared meals, baked goods, and packaged foods. The model is continuously being improved with new food categories.
Can I delete or edit entries? +
Currently, entries are automatically logged. For corrections or deletions, please contact support at support@smartcalorie.com.
